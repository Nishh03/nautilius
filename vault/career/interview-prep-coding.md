# Interview Prep - Coding

Still a normal software interview. The ML does not exempt me from this.

- Arrays, hashmaps, two pointers, sliding window - the bulk of what gets asked
- Trees and graphs to a basic level
- Time and space complexity out loud, always
- Pandas manipulation under time pressure, which is its own skill

Three problems a week, consistently, beats forty in a panic week. Talking while
solving is the part I need to practise - silent solving reads as stuck.
