# Networking and Community

The uncomfortable one, and the highest-leverage.

Referred applications convert far better than cold ones. Everything here beats
another hour of tutorial.

- Write up each project publicly. A post explaining a decision is a portfolio
  piece and a conversation starter at once.
- Answer questions in ML communities. Explaining something is the fastest way to
  find out I do not understand it.
- Contribute a small fix to a library I actually use
- Ask for 20-minute conversations, not jobs. People say yes to conversations.
