# Companies to Watch

Three tiers, deliberately.

**Startups (10-50 people)** - broadest learning, weakest mentorship. Most likely
to hire on a portfolio rather than a pedigree. My realistic entry point.

**Scale-ups** - real ML infrastructure, actual senior engineers to learn from.
The sweet spot if I can reach it.

**Big tech** - best training, hardest entry, longest process. Worth applying to
for interview practice regardless of odds.

Also: AI teams inside non-tech companies - banks, retail, healthcare. Less
glamorous, far less competition, and the data problems are often more
interesting.
