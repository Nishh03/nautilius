# Probability and Statistics

Where my intuition is worst and where interview questions concentrate.

- **Distributions** - normal, bernoulli, binomial. Which shape fits which data.
- **Bayes theorem** - updating a belief with evidence. The mental model behind
  most "why is the model confident" questions.
- **Expectation and variance** - the bias-variance framing in
  [[Overfitting and Regularisation]] comes straight from here.
- **Sampling** - why a model trained on a skewed sample fails quietly.

Common interview trap: confusing P(A given B) with P(B given A). The
medical-test example is the one to have ready.
