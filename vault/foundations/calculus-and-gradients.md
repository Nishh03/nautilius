# Calculus and Gradients

A derivative answers one question: if I nudge this input, how much does the
output move?

A **gradient** is that question asked about every input at once. It points in
the direction of steepest increase, which is why gradient *descent* goes the
opposite way.

The **chain rule** is the whole of [[Backpropagation]]. A network is functions
inside functions, and the chain rule is how a derivative gets through all of
them. Learning it properly once made backprop stop being magic.
