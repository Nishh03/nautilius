# Weekly Review Template

Every Sunday, 20 minutes. Copy this into a new note.

**What did I actually finish?** Not "worked on" — finished.

**What did I not understand this week?** Name it precisely. A vague "I don't
get transformers" becomes "I don't get why Q and K are separate matrices",
which is answerable.

**What did I avoid?** Usually maths or writeups. Avoidance is the signal.

**One thing to change next week.** One. Not five.
