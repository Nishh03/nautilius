# Six Month Plan

The goal: be employable as an AI engineer by the end of February 2027, starting
from a CS undergrad background with decent Python and rusty maths.

Not "learn AI" — that is not a goal, it has no finish line. The finish line is:
four portfolio projects that work, deployed, with writeups, and the ability to
explain every design choice in an interview.

|---|---|---|

| Oct 2026 | [[Month 2 - Classical ML]] | [[Project 1 - Churn Predictor]] deployed |
| Nov 2026 | [[Month 3 - Deep Learning]] | [[Project 2 - Image Classifier]] deployed |
| Dec 2026 | [[Month 4 - LLMs and RAG]] | [[Project 3 - RAG Assistant]] deployed |
| Jan 2027 | [[Month 5 - MLOps]] | All three projects containerised and monitored |
| Feb 2027 | [[Month 6 - Job Search]] | 40 applications out, 5 interviews done |

The rule I keep breaking and need to stop breaking: **ship the project before
starting the next month.** An unfinished project teaches less than a finished
worse one.
