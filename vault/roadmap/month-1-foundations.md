# Month 1 - Foundations

September 2026. The month I am most tempted to skip and should not.

Every ML explanation that has ever confused me turned out to be a maths gap
wearing a costume. Four weeks fixing that is cheaper than six months of
half-understanding.

- Week 1-2: [[Linear Algebra for ML]] — vectors, matrices, dot products, what a
  matrix multiplication *does* geometrically
- Week 2-3: [[Calculus and Gradients]] — derivatives, chain rule, why gradients
  point uphill
- Week 3-4: [[Probability and Statistics]] — distributions, Bayes, expectation
- Throughout: [[NumPy and Pandas Patterns]] until they are muscle memory

Success test: I can implement linear regression with gradient descent in raw
NumPy, no sklearn, and explain every line.
